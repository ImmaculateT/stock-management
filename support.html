Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new inventory item?
Go to Inventory → click Add stock item. Fill in the required fields (name, SKU, category, quantity, price) and save. The item appears in the table immediately.
Why is my order status not updating?
Order status updates require a connection to the API. If the red banner at the top of the page says the server is unreachable, click Retry after your connection is restored. You can also check the Orders page and edit the status manually.
What is the difference between "Low stock" and "Out of stock"?
Low stock means the quantity is at or below the item's threshold (default 10). Out of stock means the quantity is zero. Both appear in the inventory stats strip.
How do I export my data?
Every table (Inventory, Orders, Support) has an Export CSV button in the toolbar. The file is named with the date so you can keep historical snapshots.
Can I recover a deleted item?
Deletions are permanent from the UI. If you need recovery, contact support with the item SKU or order ID — we keep server-side backups for 30 days.
