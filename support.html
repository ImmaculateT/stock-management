Frequently asked questions

How do I contact support?
Use the support form on this page. Gmail will open with your message ready to send. You can also write directly to maricopaconalied@gmail.com.
How long does it take to receive a response?
Our team aims to respond within 24–48 working hours. Urgent issues are prioritised and usually answered the same day.
What should I include in my support request?
Include a clear description of the issue, any order or SKU numbers involved, and steps you've already tried. Screenshots help too.
Can I get help with an order that hasn't arrived?
Yes. Include the order ID (starts with ord_) and the delivery date in the form. We'll trace it with the courier and update you.
